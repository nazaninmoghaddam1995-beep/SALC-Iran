# SALC Iran
## Product Concept & Development Documentation

**Project Name:** SALC Iran  
**Project Type:** Online Self-Access Language Learning Platform  
**Creator:** Nazanin Moghaddam 
**Initial Documentation Date:** October 2026  
**Current Version:** 1.0  

---

## 1. Project Overview

SALC Iran is an online self-access language learning platform designed
to help language learners study independently while having access to
structured resources, guided learning paths, speaking practice,
community activities, and learning support.

The platform is designed to provide an experience beyond a traditional
language course by combining independent learning with opportunities
for practice, guidance, and community interaction.

---

## 2. Vision

The vision of SALC Iran is to create a flexible and accessible
environment where learners can take greater control of their own
language learning journey.

SALC aims to connect independent study, structured resources,
real-world practice, learning guidance, and community-based learning
within one platform.

---

## 3. Core Principles

- Learner autonomy
- Flexible learning
- Level-based learning paths
- Self-directed study
- Practical language use
- Community-based practice
- Accessible learning resources
- Personalized learning support

---

## 4. Current Platform Structure

The current platform includes:

- Home
- About
- Courses
- English Levels
- A1 Learning Path
- A2 Learning Path
- B1 Learning Path
- B2 Learning Path
- Learning Resources
- Learning Guides
- Events & Community

Each English level provides access to level-appropriate learning
resources and additional learning opportunities.

---

## 5. Learning Journey

The intended learner journey is:

1. Choose a language
2. Identify the appropriate level
3. Explore learning resources
4. Study independently
5. Practice through speaking activities
6. Participate in community activities
7. Get learning guidance when needed
8. Continue and adjust the learning journey

---

## 6. Core Learning Areas

SALC currently organizes learning around:

- Listening
- Reading
- Speaking
- Grammar & Vocabulary

The platform may expand these areas with additional forms of practice
and personalized learning support.

---

## 7. Speaking & Community

SALC is designed to provide opportunities for learners to practice
language with other learners.

Potential formats include:

- Weekly Speaking Rooms
- Language Gatherings
- Workshops
- Special Events
- Community Activities

The platform may use external communication tools such as Google Meet
for online speaking sessions.

---

## 8. Learning Guidance

SALC is not intended to provide only a collection of resources.

Learning Guides are intended to help learners:

- choose appropriate resources
- understand what to study
- create a realistic study plan
- organize independent learning
- identify areas that need more practice

---

## 9. Future Development

Potential future features include:

- Personalized study plans
- AI-assisted learning support
- AI language practice
- Writing feedback
- Speaking practice and role-play
- Personalized exercises
- Progress tracking
- Learner accounts
- Resource management
- Community features

- Membership options

---

## 10. Target Users

SALC is primarily designed for language learners who:

- prefer independent learning
- need flexibility
- want structured resources
- want opportunities to practice speaking
- need guidance but do not necessarily want traditional
  classroom-based courses
- want to combine self-study with community learning

---

## 11. Product Direction

SALC Iran is intended to develop from a static educational website
into a broader digital learning platform.

The long-term goal is to create an integrated learning environment
where learners can independently explore, practice, receive guidance,
and build their own learning journey.

---

## 12. Development Status

**Current Stage:** Early MVP / Prototype

The current version focuses on establishing the information
architecture, learning paths, resources, and user journey.

Future development will focus on interactive functionality,
personalization, community features, and sustainable monetization.
