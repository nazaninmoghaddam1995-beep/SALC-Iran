# SALC Iran
## Product Features & Structure

**Creator:** Nazanin Moghaddam
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** October 2026

---

## 1. Core Product

SALC Iran is an online self-access language learning platform
designed around learner autonomy, structured learning paths,
independent study, practice, guidance, and community learning.

---

## 2. Learning Path Structure

The platform organizes learning through structured levels.

Current English levels:

- A1 — Beginner
- A2 — Elementary
- B1 — Intermediate
- B2 — Upper-Intermediate

Each level has its own learning page and learning resources.

---

## 3. Learning Areas

Each level is organized around four main learning areas:

- Listening
- Reading
- Speaking
- Grammar & Vocabulary

---

## 4. Resource-Based Learning

SALC provides learners with organized access to external and
internal learning resources.

Resources are organized according to:

- Language
- Level
- Learning area
- Learning purpose

---

## 5. Speaking Rooms

SALC is designed to provide recurring opportunities for learners
to practice speaking with other learners.

The intended structure includes:

- Level-based Speaking Rooms
- Regular online sessions
- External meeting platforms such as Google Meet
- Community-based language practice

---

## 6. Learning Guides

The Learning Guides section is designed to help learners make
better use of the platform.

It may include:

- Resource selection
- Study guidance
- Study planning
- Learning strategies
- Personalized recommendations

---

## 7. Events & Community

SALC includes community-oriented learning opportunities such as:

- Speaking Rooms
- Language Gatherings
- Workshops
- Special Events
- Community Activities

---

## 8. Learner Journey

The core learner journey is:

Choose → Explore → Study → Practice → Get Guidance → Continue

The platform is designed to allow learners to move through this
journey according to their individual needs.

---

## 9. Future Product Features

Planned or potential features include:

- Progress Tracking
- Learner Accounts

## 10. Product Evolution

SALC Iran is intended to evolve from an initial website prototype
into a broader digital learning platform.

The product structure and features may change as the platform is
tested with real learners and new requirements are identified.

Major changes should be documented in the development history.
