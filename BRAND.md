## 1. Brand Name

SALC stands for:

Self-Access Language Learning Centre

The brand name reflects the project's focus on learner autonomy,
independent learning, accessible resources, practice, guidance,
and community-based language learning.

---

## 2. Brand

The official project brand is:

SALC Iran

The brand represents the digital language learning platform,
its website, learning pathways, resources, community activities,
and future digital learning services.

---

## 3. Product Description

SALC Iran is an online self-access language learning platform
that helps learners build and manage their own language learning
journey through structured resources, practice opportunities,
learning guidance, and community interaction.

---

## 4. Brand Direction

SALC Iran aims to create a learning experience that is:

- Independent
- Flexible
- Structured
- Practical
- Community-oriented
- Accessible
- Learner-centered
