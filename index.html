THE SALC PHILOSOPHY

Learning autonomy starts with choice.

Effective independent learning is built on more than access to resources. It requires ownership, flexibility and the confidence to make learning decisions.

01

Learner Autonomy

Take an active role in setting goals and making decisions about your learning.

02

Flexibility

Explore different materials and approaches according to your interests and needs.

03

Active Engagement

Build knowledge through curiosity, exploration and meaningful practice.

04

Guided Independence

Access guidance when needed while maintaining ownership of your learning.
