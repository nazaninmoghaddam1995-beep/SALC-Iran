Learning Beyond the Classroom.

SALC Iran is a self-access language learning space built around learner autonomy, meaningful practice and access to the right support at the right time.

Why SALC?

Traditional language learning often follows one fixed path: one class, one schedule, one teacher and one pace.

But learners are different. They have different goals, interests, schedules, strengths and ways of learning.

SALC was created to offer another possibility: a space where learners can take a more active role in deciding how they learn.

Not Just a Course

SALC is not built around a single course or textbook. Learners can explore different resources and activities based on their needs.

Not Learning Alone

Self-access learning does not mean isolation. Speaking rooms, events and community activities create opportunities to learn with others.

Not Without Guidance

Learner autonomy does not mean having to figure everything out alone. Learning Guides can help learners choose resources and create a direction.

Not One-Size-Fits-All

Different learners need different paths. SALC brings together resources, practice opportunities and guidance in one space.

How SALC Works

SALC brings together four parts of the learning journey.

01 · Explore

Discover resources, activities and learning opportunities that match your interests and level.

02 · Choose

Decide what you want to work on and build a learning routine that fits your goals.

03 · Practice

Use your language through speaking, listening, reading, writing and community activities.

04 · Reflect & Grow

Track your progress, adjust your approach and get guidance when you need it.

Who Is SALC For?

SALC is for learners who want to take an active role in their language learning.

Independent Learners

You enjoy exploring resources and deciding what you want to learn next.

Busy Learners

You need a flexible way to learn around your own schedule.

Curious Learners

You want to learn through topics, conversations and experiences beyond textbooks.

Learners Who Need Direction

You want independence but sometimes need a teacher or guide to help you move forward.

Our Vision

We want to build a learning environment where access to resources, community and guidance makes independent language learning more possible.
