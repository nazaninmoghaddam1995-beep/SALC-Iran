About SALC Iran

SALC Iran is a self-access language learning space designed for learners who want more freedom, flexibility and control over their learning journey.

What is SALC?

SALC stands for Self-Access Language Learning Centre. It is a learning environment where learners can choose what, how and when they want to study.

Learn Independently

SALC encourages learners to become more independent by exploring resources, setting goals and building their own learning routines.

Learn with Others

Independent learning does not mean learning alone. SALC also creates opportunities for speaking, discussion, events and community practice.

Get Guidance When You Need It

Learners can also connect with a SALC Learning Guide when they need help choosing resources, creating a study plan or staying on track.

Our Approach

We believe language learning should be flexible, meaningful and connected to the learner's own goals.

Choice

Learners should have different resources, activities and ways to practice.

Autonomy

Learners should gradually become confident in making decisions about their own learning.

Community

Language grows through communication, interaction and shared experiences.

Guidance

Independent learners can still benefit from support, feedback and a clear direction.

Learning Is Your Journey

Explore resources, practice with others, find your direction and build a learning routine that works for you.
