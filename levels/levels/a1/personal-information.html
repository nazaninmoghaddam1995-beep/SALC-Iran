A1 · Personal Information & Introductions

Let's Introduce Ourselves!

In this activity, you will learn useful words and expressions for introducing yourself in English.

Learning goal: Introduce yourself and ask simple questions about another person.

Part 1: Learn the Words

Name: My name is Sara.
Age: I am 20 years old.
Country: I am from Iran.
City: I live in Kerman.
Language: I speak Persian.

Read the examples. Then choose the correct answer.

Part 2: Check Your Understanding

1. "My name is Sara." What information does Sara give?

2. "I am from Iran." What does this tell us?

3. Which sentence tells us where someone lives?

4. Which sentence tells us about a language?

Part 3: Useful Questions

Read these questions and answers aloud.

What's your name?
My name is Sara.

Where are you from?
I'm from Iran.

Where do you live?
I live in Kerman.

What language do you speak?
I speak Persian.

Part 4: Your Turn!

Complete these sentences about yourself. You can write your answers in a notebook.

My name is __________.
I am from __________.
I live in __________.
I speak __________.

Speaking challenge: Say your four sentences aloud without reading them.
